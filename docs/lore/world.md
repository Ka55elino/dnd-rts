## Foundation of the universe

The world arose from **primal energy**, the fundamental component of the universe from which all that exists began. Its concentrations became the centers around which the world took shape.

How exactly primal energy relates to magic has not been determined yet.

## Main conflict

The mechanics already tell us who is against whom (see [`../FACTIONS.md`](../FACTIONS.md)):
- **Human Empire** — stone fortresses, formations, steel;
- **High Elves** — the ancient people of the forests;
- **Dark Elves** — shadows, intrigue, poison;
- **Alkars** — a pale aristocratic civilization of strength and necrotic power; orcs are their artificially created servants (formerly the "Dark Lord's army");
- **Dwarves** — the people under the mountains;
- **Celestials** — a heavenly host (faction description is TODO).
