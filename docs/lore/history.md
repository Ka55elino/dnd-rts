## The Age of Unification

The only era before the events of the game.

The peoples of Oikouménē gradually developed organized societies, established settlements and founded their first kingdoms and states. Tribes and clans united under rulers, religious institutions and shared traditions, while trade, territorial expansion and conflicts shaped the emerging civilizations. Distinct cultures, political systems and religious beliefs took root, laying the foundations for the nations that exist at the beginning of the game.

- **Duration:** about 500 years.
- **How it ended:** with the world taking the shape it has at the start of the game and with the founding of the first great cities.
- **What remains of it:** the civilizations themselves and their cities. The peoples of the game are the very first civilizations of Oikouménē, and their cities are the very first cities; there is no older lost civilization and there are no ruins of earlier peoples.

## The present — start of the game: first contact

The factions know about each other, but have not yet interacted with one another in any way. The main source of the coming conflict is religion (see [`world.md`](world.md) and [`religions.md`](religions.md)).

- **What brings the peoples into contact:** exploration — the peoples simply find each other.
- **Balance of power:** who is strong, who has weakened. > ❓

## Old grudges and alliances

The peoples meet for the first time at the start of the game, so there are no grudges or alliances between them yet: all conflicts and alliances arise during the game.

The High Elves and the Dark Elves are not related, despite their similarity: they meet for the first time too.
