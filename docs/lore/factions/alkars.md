# Alkars

Status: `draft`

Mechanics and roster: [`../../FACTIONS.md`](../../FACTIONS.md) §5.

The **Alkars** are an aristocratic civilization of strength and beauty, governed by the strongest of its members, with artificially created orcs as its labor force and a grim necropolis city on the edge of its culture.

## Appearance

- **Skin.** Completely pale, white or porcelain. This is the main distinguishing feature of the Alkars. No ordinary human skin tone appears even in those who spend a lot of time in the sun.
- **Necrotic lines.** In some, greenish veins or thin branching lines show through under the skin. They can be barely noticeable or distinct. This is simply a physiological trait, like the color of veins; it is not tied to age, power or social standing.

## Society and power

### Aristocracy

Alkar society consists exclusively of aristocrats. This is an unusual model of a state: it has no familiar layer of common Alkar citizens.

Possible consequences of such a system:

- An Alkar's standing is determined by their place among the aristocracy.
- Rich clothing, outward beauty and displays of strength become part of social life.
- Prestige can be expressed through residences, personal estates, jewelry and the ability to keep one's own servants.
- Relations between aristocrats can be built on rivalry, recognition of strength and the struggle for influence.

If everyone belongs to the elite, the social hierarchy does not disappear — it becomes a struggle within the privileged class.

### A single ruler

At the head stands a single ruler, the strongest among the Alkars.

Power is justified not only by lineage or inheritance, but also by personal superiority.

### Who is the strongest

Among the Alkars, strength is not only physical might. The strongest is the one who can bend others to their will and hold on to power despite constant attempts to challenge it.

- **The right to challenge.** Any warrior may contest a commander's position if they can prove their superiority.
- **Strength is not the same as rank.** An experienced general may be weaker than a young warrior, yet keep power through strategy, the loyalty of subordinates and victories.
- **Defeat has a price.** The loser does not necessarily die, but loses authority, and sometimes the right to command.
- **Strength is proven by results.** A warrior who wins duels but ruins their own squad is not necessarily worthy of a high position.
- **An alliance of the strongest.** The highest commanders may hold power not alone, but through alliances, mutual obligations and fear of a common enemy.

## Necrotic power

Like everyone else's magic, the Alkars' necrotic power is one of the primordial energies (see [`../magic.md`](../magic.md)). The Alkars simply were more strongly under its influence than other peoples.

## Orcs — an artificially created labor force

Here orcs are not just another people on equal footing among the neighbors, but artificially created beings used as servants and workers. Their functions:

- serving the palaces and estates of the aristocrats;
- building and maintaining the city infrastructure;
- doing the everyday work the elite needs in order to exist.

Goblins and trolls are created the same way as orcs. Harpies are not created: they are wild winged creatures attracted or subjugated by the Alkars.

The knowledge of how to create orcs is kept by the order of mages of Alkaris, headed by the Dark Mage. Orcs and the other created creatures are made from stone and wood with the help of dark energy; the details of the process are not important for the lore.

## Where they live

See [`../geography.md`](../geography.md): the city of **Alkaris** in the east, beyond the ridge, and the **Alkarian Necropolis** in the northeast.

## Gray side

The Alkars believe that life and death are parts of an eternal cycle in which every ending makes way for a new beginning. They respect the dead and care for the dying, seeing death not as an enemy but as a necessary part of existence. However, their acceptance of this cycle can make them appear cruel and indifferent to those who cannot accept loss. They may refuse to risk the lives of many to save a few, believing that no individual life should take precedence over the continuity of the whole. To the Alkars, this is acceptance of nature's order; to others, it is a cold justification for abandoning those who could still be saved.

Another "gray" side: the Faith of the Eternal Cycle reveres death as a natural part of the cycle, yet allows breaking the cycle to maintain balance — hence the raised dead, the Wraith King and the necropolis (see [`../religions.md`](../religions.md)).

There are no aristocrats opposing the system and no self-aware orcs yet; this may become a hook for the campaign (see [`../campaign.md`](../campaign.md)).

## Faith

**The Faith of the Eternal Cycle**: life and death are parts of one process; communities of keepers of the cycle instead of a church. Full description: [`../religions.md`](../religions.md#alkars--the-faith-of-the-eternal-cycle).

## Heroes

Heroes are titles, not specific characters. In the campaign each title will belong to different characters; in PvP heroes are simply units. Names, backgrounds and personal stories are not written in the lore.

All three titles belong to Alkars (pale aristocrats).

### Dark Lord

Lord of war. The ruler of the Alkars, the strongest of them. His power rests on personal superiority: he stays on the throne as long as no one has surpassed him.

### Dark Mage

Fire and acid. The head of the order of mages, based in the center of Alkaris. The order is the keeper of the knowledge of how to create orcs: the Alkars' entire labor force depends on it.

### Wraith King

Lord of the dead. The warden of the Alkarian Necropolis, the one and only guardian who watches over the entire city of the dead. He raises the fallen (ghosts and skeletons in the game). He himself has already become one of the dead he guards.

## Units

The Alkars have one skin: `alkaris` (see [`../../ASSETS.md`](../../ASSETS.md), Regional skins). Concept art: [`../images/alkars/units/`](../images/alkars/units/).

The Alkar army has two parts:
- **The elite — the Alkars themselves:** aristocrats serving as expensive elite units — **Alkar swordsmen**, **Alkar archers** and **dark acolytes**. No other peoples serve in the army: there are no humans among the Alkar troops.
- **Everyone else — created creatures and beasts:** the cheap, numerous core of the army — goblins (builders), orcs, berserkers, orc hunters, orc spearmen, harpies, goblin wolf riders, siege trolls.

### Goblin

*Builder.* Goblins do the main construction and household work of the Alkar army. They raise primitive fortifications, repair buildings, gather resources and maintain siege machines. They are neither strong nor hardy, but they are numerous and easily replaced.

**Appearance:** a small, wiry body, long ears, rough clothing, leather straps, tools and small trophies.

### Orc

The main fighter of the Alkars and the backbone of their military strength. Orcs have great physical strength and good endurance, can withstand long battles and fight effectively in close combat. A versatile warrior with no narrow specialization.

**Appearance:** a massive build, rough metal armor, leather elements, large weapons and a minimum of decorative details. The distinguishing feature is tusks and tribal war markings.

### Berserker

An orc devoted solely to battle. Berserkers prefer attack to defense, use heavy weapons and can deal enormous damage in close combat. Especially dangerous when assaulting fortified positions and dense formations, but their weak protection makes them vulnerable to archers.

**Appearance:** a massive body, minimal armor, war paint, bones and trophies. Large axes or heavy paired blades. Visually he should look more dangerous than an ordinary orc and a little bigger, but not like a separate species.

### Orc Hunter

Orc hunters specialize in tracking down and destroying individual targets. They use heavy hunting spears and prefer mobility and distance to direct confrontation. Well suited to fighting light infantry and scouts.

**Appearance:** lightened leather armor, fur elements, animal trophies, hunting weapons and distinctive markings on the face.

### Harpy

Wild winged creatures attracted or subjugated by the Alkars. Harpies attack from the air, can quickly overcome obstacles and strike archers and mages with crossbows. They have little protection and are poorly suited to long fights with heavy troops.

**Appearance:** a humanoid body with bird-like features, powerful wings, clawed limbs, dark feathers and a predatory silhouette with a crossbow. They do not have to be fully human-like: their bestial origin can be emphasized.

### Dark Acolyte

Acolytes study necrotic energy and serve as conduits of the Alkars' magical power. They perform rituals, sustain summoned creatures and help maintain magical bonds. Unlike a battle mage, their specialty is supporting necromantic forces, not destroying the enemy directly.

**Appearance:** dark rough robes, ritual signs and a staff or wand. Green energy lines and glow are especially visible on the hands and magical items.

### Siege Troll

A huge creature the Alkars use to storm fortifications. The troll has colossal physical strength and can destroy walls, gates and buildings with heavy blows. It is also a serious threat to ordinary infantry, but it is slow and vulnerable to concentrated attacks.

**Appearance:** a massive body, rough skin, disproportionately large hands, primitive protection of iron and bones.

### Wraith

*Summoned.* An incorporeal creature summoned through necrotic magic. Wraiths move on the ground like ordinary warriors, attack the enemy's life energy with necrotic damage and inspire fear.

**Appearance:** a translucent humanoid silhouette, ragged dark shapes instead of clothing, elongated limbs and a green or pale glow inside the body. It must not look like an ordinary skeleton in a cloak.

### Skeletons

*Summoned.* Animated remains used as cheap, expendable infantry. Skeletons feel no fear and need no rest, but are weaker and less durable than full-fledged fighters. Their value lies in the ability to create numerous squads and distract the enemy. There are two types:

- **Skeleton Swordsman** — melee.
- **Skeleton Archer** — ranged, can also hit air targets.

**Appearance:** bones in various states, swords or primitive bows, remnants of old armor. A green glow in the eye sockets or on individual bones shows the magical bond with the summoner.

### Demon

*Summoned by the Dark Mage.* A powerful creature from an unknown source that the Dark Mage can summon through a complex ritual. The demon far surpasses ordinary infantry in strength and is a threat even to elite units. Its summoning must be expensive and limited, so that a demon's appearance feels like an important event on the battlefield.

**Appearance:** a large humanoid or beast-like body, horns, claws, dark skin or a stone-like surface. Green energy cracks and an unstable glow emphasize its alien nature.
