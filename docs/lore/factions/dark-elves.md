# Dark Elves

Status: `draft`

Mechanics and roster: [`../../FACTIONS.md`](../../FACTIONS.md) §4.

## Concept (from the mechanics)

A people of shadows and intrigue: sabotage, ambushes, poison, webs and necrotic power. They win not head-on, but by weakening the enemy before the battle.

## Who they are

- **What they call themselves and what others call them:** **Shaessari**; others call them drow.
- **Where they came from, how old they are as a people:** one of the first civilizations of Oikouménē; took shape during the Age of Unification (about 500 years, see [`../history.md`](../history.md)).
- **Where they live** (see [`../geography.md`](../geography.md)): underground (the Underdark). Melasarach is the religious center with the temple of the Great Spider; Aterliman is a neutral underground port at the Nimrath Sea. There are no other drow settlements.
- **How society works** (power, estates, faith): a matriarchy — women head the noble houses. In Melasarach, power rests on the cult of the Great Spider, headed by its high priestess, the Spider Queen (the high priestesses are both a spiritual and a political force; the closer a house is to the temple, the higher its status). In Aterliman the matriarchy is secular: power belongs to a council of noble families, by inheritance, wealth and trade; the priesthood gives no political advantage

- **Their "gray" side** (mixed tone): see [Gray side](#gray-side) below.

## Gray side

The Dark Elves believe that strength, discipline, and devotion to the Spider Queen are the foundations of civilization. Their society has achieved remarkable unity and stability, but this order comes at the cost of individual freedom. Religious authorities use fear and prophecy to justify political purges, while rival houses exploit faith to strengthen their own power. Some sincerely believe their harsh traditions protect their people from chaos; others use them to maintain control.

## Faith

**The Cult of the Great Spider**: the world is a great Weave spun by the Great Spider; the Spider Queen is its high priestess; the cult is tied to the matriarchy, and priestesses hold spiritual and political power. Full description: [`../religions.md`](../religions.md#dark-elves--the-cult-of-the-great-spider).

## Relations

At the start of the game the peoples meet for the first time (first contact, see [`../history.md`](../history.md)): there are no established relations yet. Attitudes toward other peoples follow from the faith (see "Faith" above) and arise during the game.

## Heroes

Heroes are titles, not specific characters. In the campaign each title will belong to different characters; in PvP heroes are simply units. Names, backgrounds and personal stories are not written in the lore.

### Dark Paladin

Necrotic warrior.

### Dark Archer

Huntress from the shadows.

### Spider Queen

Mistress of webs, takes the form of a spider. The high priestess of the Great Spider and head of the cult in Melasarach. A living being like everyone else, only endowed with far greater power: she can fight, she can be wounded and defeated.

### Webweaver

The builder of the Shaessari. A specialist who uses spider silk and special materials to create structures, fortifications and building elements.

**Appearance:**
- dark clothing with spider ornaments;
- tools for working with webs;
- emphasizes the religious and cultural character of the Shaessari.

### Blade Dancer

A trained warrior who turns combat into a sequence of fast, precise movements. Blade dancers use paired curved swords, dodge heavy attacks and try to get behind the enemy. Especially dangerous to archers and slow units, but vulnerable under concentrated fire.

**Appearance:** close-fitting armor of dark steel, two curved blades, asymmetric pauldrons and short strips of silk. In motion the silhouette should resemble a spider quickly moving its legs.

### Crossbowman

A disciplined shooter who uses a mechanical crossbow in each hand to strike enemies from a safe distance. Heavy bolts are dangerous to armored targets, and special ammunition can be used to weaken enemies. The crossbowman reloads more slowly and copes poorly with attacks at close range.

**Appearance:** dark leather armor, compact crossbows with metal parts, a supply of bolts and a closed helmet or mask. Elite shooters may have mechanisms decorated with silver.

### Scout

Scouts operate ahead of the main army, study the enemy's positions, detect hidden squads and choose routes for flanking attacks. In battle they prefer to avoid direct confrontation, relying on speed and surprise.

**Appearance:** light black clothing, a hood or mask, small daggers, gear for moving across rocks and caves. Unlike the blade dancer, the scout's equipment is as functional as possible and almost free of decoration.

### Spider Rider

Riders on giant spiders can move across difficult terrain and attack where ordinary cavalry is powerless. They go around enemy positions, pursue archers and can slow enemies with webs. Their advantage is not a direct breakthrough by force, but control of space and unexpected attacks.

**Appearance:** a large dark spider with long jointed legs, an obsidian carapace and prominent eyes. The rider is secured in a light saddle or special harness and uses a short spear or a blade. The spider must be recognizable even at a small model scale.

### Dark Priest

A servant of the Shaessari religious tradition who performs the rites of the cult of the Great Spider, headed by the Spider Queen. Supports allies, weakens enemies and disrupts the enemy's battle order with curses. In Melasarach priests hold considerable political influence, while in Aterliman religious functions may remain part of the tradition of the noble houses.

**Appearance:** long black and burgundy robes, silver ornaments, web symbolism and a ritual staff. Visually the priest must differ from the battle mage: their magic looks ceremonial, based on signs, seals and thin threads of energy.

### Dark Mage

A battle mage who studies forbidden ways of affecting life energy. Uses destructive spells, curses and necrotic pulses that weaken enemies. Their abilities come from the magical tradition of the Shaessari and do not make them a representative of the Alkars.

**Appearance:** dark layered clothing, metal elements, runic ornaments and a staff with a purple or greenish energy focus. A green glow may run along cracks in the magical focus or along patterns on the clothing, but must not automatically mean physical changes to the body.

### Dark Dragon

A rare and dangerous creature that represents the peak of the Shaessari's military power. The dragon can attack ground squads from the air, destroy fortifications and threaten several parts of the battlefield at once. Its high cost and vulnerability to concentrated attacks must limit the number of such creatures in an army.

**Appearance:** a massive body, black or graphite scales, wide wings, curved horns and purple glowing eyes. Silvery bony ridges and spider motifs in the shape of the horns or the natural pattern of the scales may be added. The dragon should not be turned into a mechanical or fully armored monster.
