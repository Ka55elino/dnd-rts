# Dark Elves

Status: `draft`

Mechanics and roster: [`../../FACTIONS.md`](../../FACTIONS.md) §4.

## Concept (from the mechanics)

A people of shadows and intrigue: sabotage, ambushes, poison, webs and necrotic power. They win not head-on, but by weakening the enemy before the battle.

## Who they are

- **What they call themselves and what others call them:** > ❓
- **Where they came from, how old they are as a people:** > ❓
- **Where they live** (see [`../geography.md`](../geography.md)): underground (the Underdark). Melasarach is the religious center with the temple of the Spider Queen; Aterliman is a neutral underground port at the Nimrath Sea; > ❓ other settlements
- **How society works** (power, estates, faith): a matriarchy — women head the noble houses. In Melasarach, power rests on the cult of the Spider Queen (the high priestesses are both a spiritual and a political force; the closer a house is to the temple, the higher its status). In Aterliman the matriarchy is secular: power belongs to a council of noble families, by inheritance, wealth and trade; the priesthood gives no political advantage
